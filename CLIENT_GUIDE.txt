BUSINESS MANAGEMENT PLATFORM
Client Quick Guide

Website:
https://bussiness-management-project0101.vercel.app

WHAT THE WEBSITE DOES
This platform helps retail businesses manage daily work in one place.
It is simple, organised, and saves time by keeping business records together.

MAIN FEATURES
- Create a secure account and verify your email with an OTP.
- Sign in using a password and email OTP.
- Manage products, prices, stock, and product images.
- Manage customers, contact details, bills, paid amounts, and pending amounts.
- Manage suppliers and supplier payments.
- Manage agents and customer information.
- Record receivables and payables in the Finance section.
- View orders and financial summaries.
- Customers can browse available products and place orders.
- Customers can choose cash on delivery or record an online payment.
- Responsive design works on computers, tablets, and mobile phones.

HOW TO USE IT
1. Open the website.
2. Select "Create an account".
3. Enter your username, email, password, and account type.
4. Click "Send verification OTP".
5. Enter the OTP received by email and create your account.
6. Return to "Log in".
7. Enter your username and password, then request and verify the login OTP.

FOR BUSINESS OWNERS
After signing in, use the dashboard buttons to:
- Add, edit, or remove records.
- Update product stock and prices.
- Track customers, suppliers, agents, and payments.
- Review orders and financial information.

FOR CUSTOMERS
1. Sign up with the Customer account type.
2. Sign in to open the storefront.
3. Search or browse products.
4. Select "Order now".
5. Enter quantity, payment method, contact number, and delivery address.
6. Place the order.

WHY IT IS EASY TO USE
- Clear sections for each part of the business.
- Simple forms and easy-to-understand buttons.
- Search helps customers find products quickly.
- Important business totals are available in one dashboard.
- Email OTP verification adds security without making login difficult.
